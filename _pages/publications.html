Peer-reviewed papers, technical reports and short notes, grouped by the question they ask rather than by the year they appeared. Most of the work sits where ecology, statistics and conservation meet — counting animals that are hard to see, and working out what the numbers mean for rivers and forests.

A paper can belong to more than one group — a camera-trap study of density is also a population study — so several appear twice. The counts below therefore add up to more than the number of papers. Newest first within each group.
